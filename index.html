Quick start
  • Open the Images tab and load a Portrait A (and Portrait B for a second speaker), some Logo / images, and optionally an Extra window picture. You can also drag image files straight onto a box.
  • Press Voice file and choose your recording, then press Play (Space). The oscilloscope draws the live waveform and the portrait glows when the voice is loud.
  • Click a box that holds several images to step to the next one.
  • Press Export video to record the whole console plus the voice into one video file.
  • Press H (or the ☰ button on a phone) to hide the controls while presenting.
The five boxes
  • Box 1 – Speaker portrait. Switches between Portrait A and B. Glows with the voice.
  • Box 2 – Logo / images. Faction logo or mission pictures. Can be shaped (circle, shield, triangle, hexagon) and can spin like a coin.
  • Box 3 – Oscilloscope. Always a plain rectangle. Shrinks into a strip when the radar is open.
  • Box 4 – Extra window. Optional picture window above the scope (Show fourth box).
  • Box 5 – Radar. A digital grid with contacts that move on programmed courses (Show radar box (5th)).

Boxes 1, 2, 4 and 5 can each be shown or hidden with the buttons in the top row. They grow into view and shrink away with a short animation, and the video records it. The remaining boxes resize to fill the space.

Playing, pausing and exporting video
  • Play / Stop runs the voice file. Pause freezes the voice, the video recording and the radar together; make any changes you like while paused, then press Resume and they appear in the video straight away.
  • Export video starts a recording that includes the voice, then saves a video file when the voice ends. Keep this browser tab in view while exporting, or the recording freezes.
  • With Boxes open at start of recording on (Look tab, or O), the video opens on an empty screen and every box grows into view one after another before the voice starts. Turn it off for a plain start.
  • Export frame chooses the video shape: match screen uses your window's proportions, 16:9 uses a fixed widescreen frame.
Conversation tab: two speakers, auto-switch and cues
  • Give each speaker a name; the name shows on the portrait.
  • Switch speakers with 1, 2, S, the Speaker button, an Auto switch timer (every N seconds), or by recording cues.
  • Record cues: turn it on, press Play, then do things at the right moments. It records speaker switches, showing or hiding boxes 1, 2, 4 and 5, spins, and image steps. Turn recording off and play again, and everything replays at the same times (including in an exported video). Clear cues erases them.
  • Each recording remembers how things looked when you pressed Play, so the replay starts from the same state.
Portrait tab
  • Backdrop color / Backdrop image sit behind a transparent portrait.
  • Clean portrait background removes a baked-in checkerboard pattern; Strength tunes how aggressively.
  • Zoom, Left/right, Up/down frame the portrait. You can also drag the portrait in the box and use the mouse wheel to zoom. Reset position puts it back.
Logo & spin tab
  • Spin box 2 / Spin box 4 turn the logo like a 3D coin (box 4 spins as a slab).
  • Shape box 2 turns the shaping on or off. Choose Circle, Shield, Triangle or Hexagon badge.
  • Crop to fill fills the whole shape (with zoom and position sliders); Fit whole image shows the entire picture on a Fill color.
Look tab: CRT effects and colors
  • CRT lines adds scanlines (adjust Strength); CRT bevel adds the monitor frame to boxes 1, 2, 4 and 5.
  • Theme: Hue, Scope color and Background color. Reset colors returns to default.
  • Presets: four built in (Green, Amber terminal, Blue radar, Red alert). Type a name and press Save preset to keep your own; they are stored in this browser. Your last colors are remembered.
Radar: grid and controls
  • Turn the radar on with Show radar box (5th); the Radar tab appears.
  • Grid color is separate from the main theme. Grid size sets how many columns (more columns = smaller squares). Sweep shows the rotating sweep line. Snap to grid drops markers into the center of a square.
  • Each square has a reference like F7, shown under every marker's label.
  • Motion speed speeds up or slows down all movement (0.25× to 6×). Preview motion runs the radar without audio. During playback and export, contacts move with the voice. Reset contacts returns everyone to their starting spots, Clear all removes everything.
  • Your radar setup (contacts, markers and settings) is saved in this browser.
Radar: placing contacts and static positions
  • Set Place → Type, Faction (Friendly blue, Hostile red, Unknown amber) and Shape, optionally type a Label for next marker, then click or tap the radar. Drag a marker to move it; click it to edit it.
  • Moving contact: choose how it moves in the Selected row: Holds still, Straight line (set Heading in degrees, 0 = up, and Speed in cells per second; at the edge it can bounce, wrap or stop) or Follows path (turn on Add waypoints and click the grid; tick Loop to repeat).
  • Appears at makes a contact enter the radar at a chosen second of the recording.
  • Static position is a large fixed icon with a centered label, for bases, objectives and landmarks. It never moves. Size resizes any marker.
  • Shapes include circle, square, diamond, triangle, star, cross, hexagon, crosshair, base/building and flag.
Radar: combat markers
  • Place one with Type → Combat marker. By default it is hidden during the show and pops up (a ring and flash) when a contact crosses it. While editing you see it as a ghosted starburst with a dashed zone.
  • Radius sets the size of the zone. Shows chooses hidden until triggered or always visible.
  • Triggered by limits which faction can set it off (any, hostile, friendly or unknown).
  • Affects: everything inside the zone (including contacts sitting still there) or only the contact that crosses.
  • Contact decides the outcome: becomes a kill mark (an X is left behind) or disappears.
  • For each contact, At combat markers can be Is hit (default), Survives (the marker still pops but this contact lives) or Ignores them (passes through without triggering).
  • Contacts that are not moving never set a marker off, and static positions are never affected. Every run starts fresh with all markers hidden again.
On a phone or tablet
  • Tap to place, drag to move, tap a marker to edit it. The ☰ button (top right) hides or shows the controls; with controls hidden, taps on the radar do not add markers.
  • Swipe the button row and the tab row sideways to reach everything. The panel under the tabs scrolls.
  • The grid defaults to a chunkier size on small screens; change it with Grid size.
Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing in a text box and when Ctrl, Cmd or Alt is held.

Good to know
  • Presets, last colors and the radar setup live in this browser, so they stay on this computer and browser.
  • Keep the browser tab in view while exporting video.
  • The patch lettering uses Impact if your computer has it.
